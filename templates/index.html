Workflow

  • Input image is resized to 32×32 RGB, flattened, and normalized to [0, 1].
  • PCA reduces feature dimension to 4 principal components.
  • Features are encoded into 4 qubits via ZZFeatureMap (reps=1, linear).
  • Classification is executed by VQC with RealAmplitudes ansatz & COBYLA.
